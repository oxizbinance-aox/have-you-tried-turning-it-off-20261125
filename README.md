> Have you tried turning it off and on again? Your marriage, I mean.

The oldest IT advice in the book, aimed at a marriage instead of a router.
Every helpdesk call starts the same way — power it down, power it back up,
and hope the problem clears. This entry just points that advice at the one
thing nobody ever thinks to reboot.
